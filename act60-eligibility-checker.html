Federal tax is separate. A Puerto Rico decree does not change U.S. federal tax. For a U.S. citizen or resident alien, IRC §933(1) excludes only income from sources within Puerto Rico, and only for an individual who is a bona fide resident for the entire taxable year. Source is determined under IRC §937(b) and Treas. Reg. §1.937-2, and gain on personal property is also subject to IRC §865(g)(2)-(3) and (i)(3). U.S.-source income, including most dividends from U.S. corporations, stays taxable. This tool does not compute federal tax; verify the federal analysis with a professional. A person who takes the position that they became a bona fide resident must also give the IRS notice (IRC §937(c); Form 8898).
