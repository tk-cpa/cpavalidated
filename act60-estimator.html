Act 60 of Puerto Rico (2019) Act 38-2026 (enacted Mar 10, 2026) IRC §933 IRC §937 Treas. Reg. §1.937-1 ⚠ Dec 31, 2026 Filing Deadline
⏰
Act 38-2026 filing deadline: December 31, 2026. Individual Resident Investor applications received by December 31, 2026 at 11:59 pm (portal filing-fee timestamp) are treated as requesting the 0% rate on post-residency interest, dividends, and capital gains, with the decree expiring December 31, 2035 (DDEC Informative Bulletin 2026-004; Code §§2022.01(a), 2022.02(b)). Applications received on or after January 1, 2027 receive a 4% rate on that income through December 31, 2055 (Code §§2022.01(b), 2022.02(d)), with a 6-year prior-residency lookback and a Property Registry requirement.

Excluded from federal gross income for a bona fide PR resident. IRC §933(1); Treas. Reg. §1.937-2(i).

Capital Gains (Code §2022.02)
Appreciation after you became a PR resident (e.g., value on the first day of residency to sale). Treated as PR-source for federal purposes when sold by a bona fide PR resident. Reg. §1.937-2(b), (f)(1)(vi). Excludes gain on U.S. real property.
Appreciation accrued before you became a PR resident on securities and similar assets. The sourcing rule covers property of a kind described in IRC §731(c)(3)(C)(i) or §954(c)(1)(B) owned before the move (Treas. Reg. §1.937-2(f)(1)(ii)).
Within 10 years the gain is U.S.-source (Reg. §1.937-2(f)(1)) and PR applies its regular Code (Act 60 §2022.02(a), (c)). After 10 years it is PR-source and PR taxes it at 5%.
Business & Earned Income
Net income of a PR business holding an Export Services decree (Code §§2031.01, 2032.01(a): 4% fixed rate; distributions exempt under §2032.01(e)).
Wages or self-employment income for services performed in the U.S. Not excluded: IRC §933(1) covers only PR-source income. Treas. Reg. §1.937-2(c).
Base-period limitation: the 4% rate applies only to growth over the 3-year average. Code §2032.01(d). Flag only; not modeled.

Federal rule (IRC §933(1)): A bona fide Puerto Rico resident for the entire taxable year excludes Puerto Rico-source income from federal gross income, with no deduction or credit allocable to it.
