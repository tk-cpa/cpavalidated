AMT Adjustments & Preference Items (Form 6251 Part I)

If you itemize: Schedule A line 7 (state and local taxes after the SALT cap, plus other taxes). If you take the standard deduction: enter the standard deduction (Form 1040 line 12e). IRC 56(b)(1)(A)(ii) and 56(b)(1)(D). SALT cap for the selected year is shown under Assumptions (IRC 164(b)(7)).
State and local income tax refund included on Schedule 1 (entered as a positive number here; subtracted). IRC 56(b)(1)(C).
Excess of FMV at exercise over exercise price on incentive stock options exercised and held at year end. Negative if you sold ISO stock and the AMT gain is lower than regular gain. IRC 56(b)(3).
Regular tax depreciation minus AMT depreciation (negative if AMT depreciation is larger). IRC 56(a)(1).
Net total of the remaining Form 6251 adjustments and preferences, for example private activity bond interest (IRC 57(a)(5)), excess percentage depletion (IRC 57(a)(1)), investment interest, NOL, passive activity, and QSBS (line 2h) items. Enter negative amounts as negatives.
Added back to AMTI through Form 6251 lines 1a-1b. Per the 2025 Instructions for Form 6251 (What's New). 2026 instructions are not yet published; the same treatment is assumed.
Form 6251 line 13 (adjusted net capital gain including qualified dividends, as refigured for AMT). Leave 0 if none. Triggers the Part III computation under IRC 55(b)(3).
Schedule D line 19 (Form 6251 line 14). Taxed at up to 25% in Part III. 28% rate gain is taxed at the 26%/28% AMT rates.
Computed separately on Form 1116 for AMT. IRC 59(a).
Available credit carryforward. Allowed only against regular tax in excess of tentative minimum tax. IRC 53(c).
Nonrefundable credits under subparts A, B, D, E and F of Part IV that reduce regular tax in the IRC 53(c)(1) limit. Used only for the Form 8801 limit.
Not modeled: Form 2555 foreign earned income, estates and trusts, children subject to the kiddie tax exemption cap (Rev. Proc. 2025-32 section 4.11), and corporate AMT (IRC 55(b)(2)).
