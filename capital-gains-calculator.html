Net Investment Income Tax (Form 8960)

Interest, non-qualified dividends, annuities, royalties, passive rents and passive business income. Capital gains and qualified dividends above are added automatically. IRC 1411(c)(1).
Investment interest, investment advisory-type items and other deductions properly allocable to the income. Reduces NII. IRC 1411(c)(1)(B).
Standard or itemized deduction plus any QBI deduction, so that AGI = taxable income + this amount. Default is the 2026 MFJ standard deduction (Rev. Proc. 2025-32 section 4.14: Single and MFS $16,100, HOH $24,150, MFJ $32,200). Change it for other years or status.
Amount excluded under IRC 911(a)(1), net of disallowed deductions (IRC 1411(d)). MAGI = AGI + this amount.
