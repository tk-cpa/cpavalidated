Closer Connection Test Validator

Determine if you meet the closer connection exception to US tax residency under IRC §7701(b)(3)(B). Requires a timely Form 8840. Tax years 2026-2030.

← cpavalidated.com

Tax Year

The eligibility rules are the same every year. The year only changes the Form 8840 filing date shown in the result.

Residency Facts

Are you a U.S. citizen, or were you a green card holder (lawful permanent resident) at any time during the year?
The exception is only for alien individuals who are not lawful permanent residents (IRC §7701(b)(1)(A)(i), (b)(3)(B); Form 8840 instructions).
Did you meet the Substantial Presence Test for the year?
At least 31 days in the year and at least 183 weighted days over three years (IRC §7701(b)(3)(A)). Use the SPT calculator.
Were you present in the United States on fewer than 183 days during the year?
Current year only, not the weighted total (IRC §7701(b)(3)(B)(i); Treas. Reg. §301.7701(b)-2(a)(1)). Days that the statute treats as not present are not counted.
Did you have a tax home in a foreign country for the entire year?
The tax home must exist all year and be in the same country to which you claim the closer connection (Treas. Reg. §301.7701(b)-2(c)). Tax home is generally your regular or principal place of business.
Do you have a closer connection to that foreign country than to the United States?
More significant contacts with the foreign country; see the factors below (Treas. Reg. §301.7701(b)-2(d)). Two countries are allowed only under the special rule in §301.7701(b)-2(e).
During the year, did you apply for or take other steps toward a green card, or have an adjustment of status pending?
Examples: Form I-485 filed by you, Form I-130 or I-140 filed on your behalf (IRC §7701(b)(3)(C); Treas. Reg. §301.7701(b)-2(f)).
Have you filed, or will you timely file, Form 8840 for the year?
Attach it to Form 1040-NR, or mail it to the IRS if no return is required, by the Form 1040-NR due date including extensions (Treas. Reg. §301.7701(b)-8(c); Form 8840 instructions).

Closer Connection Factors

The facts and circumstances considered include, but are not limited to (Treas. Reg. §301.7701(b)-2(d)(1)); no single factor is determinative:
  • Permanent home: a dwelling available at all times, continuously, and not solely for short stays; owned or rented does not matter
  • Family: where your family is located
  • Personal belongings: automobiles, furniture, clothing, and jewelry
  • Organizations: social, political, cultural, or religious organizations with which you have a current relationship
  • Banking: where you conduct routine personal banking
  • Business activities: other than those that constitute your tax home
  • Driver's license: the jurisdiction that issued it
  • Voting: the jurisdiction in which you vote
  • Country of residence designated on forms and documents
  • Official forms filed such as Form W-8 or Form W-9

Authority: IRC §7701(b)(3)(B), (C); Treas. Reg. §301.7701(b)-2 and §301.7701(b)-8; Form 8840 and its instructions
