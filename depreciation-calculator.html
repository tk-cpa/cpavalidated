Property Information

GDS periods: 168(c). ADS periods: 168(g)(2)(C) (30 years residential rental for property placed in service after 2017, 40 years nonresidential), 168(g)(3)(B) (QIP 20 years). ADS is required for property used predominantly outside the United States, 168(g)(1)(A).
The month placed in service drives the mid-month first-year percentage. 168(d)(2). Placed in service means ready and available for its specific use, not the closing date. Treas. Reg. 1.46-3(d).
Selects the year highlighted in the schedule and the year whose deduction and adjusted basis are reported.
Purchase price plus capitalized closing costs and improvements. 26 U.S.C. 1012; 263(a).
Land is not depreciable. 26 U.S.C. 167(a); Treas. Reg. 1.167(a)-2. Use an appraisal or the assessor ratio.
Used only for the adjusted basis line. Adjusted basis is cost less depreciation allowed or allowable. 26 U.S.C. 1016(a)(2).
QIP is not nonresidential real property, so the mid-month convention does not apply. Half-year unless more than 40% of the year's MACRS basis (excluding real property) was placed in service in the last quarter. 168(d)(1), (d)(3).

Bonus Depreciation - QIP Only

The election out is made by class of property for the year. 168(k)(7).
100% for property acquired and placed in service after January 19, 2025. 168(k)(1) as amended by P.L. 119-21 sec. 70301. Earlier acquisitions follow the former phase-down: 100% through 2022, then 80% (2023), 60% (2024), 40% (2025), 20% (2026), none after 2026. Rev. Proc. 2026-15, sec. 2.02.
