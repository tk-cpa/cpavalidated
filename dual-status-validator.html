Dual-Status Tax Year Validator 2026-2030

Work out whether a tax year is a dual-status year, your residency starting and termination dates, the resident and nonresident day counts, and which return to file, for years 2026 through 2030.

How this validator works

You are dual status when you are a resident alien for part of a calendar year and a nonresident alien for the rest, which usually happens in the year of arrival or departure. Enter the facts below; the result updates as you type. All tests here are statutory and are not indexed for inflation, so only the calendar (leap years and weekdays) changes from one year to the next. Dates must fall inside the selected tax year.

If you are a resident in any part of the following year, the residency termination date is December 31. IRC §7701(b)(2)(B)(iii).

Residency Starting Date

Residency under the substantial presence test starts on the first day present. IRC §7701(b)(2)(A)(iii); Treas. Reg. §301.7701(b)-4(a).
De minimis presence: up to 10 days in total, only if tax home and closer connection were abroad for those days and you file the required statement. Treas. Reg. §301.7701(b)-4(c)(1); IRC §7701(b)(2)(C).
Used only if you disregard days. You cannot disregard part of a consecutive period of presence. Treas. Reg. §301.7701(b)-4(c)(1).

6

Marital Status (for the Election Note)

Marriage does not create dual status. It only matters for the election to be treated as a resident for the entire year. IRC §6013(g), (h).
