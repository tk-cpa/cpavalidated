How this checker works

FATCA (Form 8938) and FBAR (FinCEN Form 114) are separate requirements with different thresholds, different value measures, and different filing channels. Form 8938 is attached to your income tax return; the FBAR is filed electronically with FinCEN and is never attached to a return. This checker is for individuals. Foreign Account and Asset Values (USD)

Convert each foreign-currency amount to US dollars first. Round to whole dollars.

Financial accounts at foreign institutions in which you have a financial interest or signature authority. Add the maximum value reached during the calendar year in each account; report is required if the aggregate exceeds $10,000 at any time. FBAR counts accounts only.
Specified foreign financial assets: foreign financial accounts plus foreign non-account assets held for investment (foreign stock and securities, interests in foreign entities, certain contracts with non-US counterparties). IRC §6038D(b).
The highest aggregate value of those assets at any time during the tax year. It cannot be lower than the year-end value.
