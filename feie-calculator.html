Important: This calculation assumes you qualify under the Physical Presence Test or Bona Fide Residence Test with a foreign tax home under IRC §911 and that you elect the exclusion on Form 2555. Self-employment tax is computed without the §911(a)(1) exclusion (IRC §1402(a)(11)), unless a totalization agreement says otherwise. Excluded income is still stacked at the top of the rate brackets for the remaining income (IRC §911(f)), no credit or deduction is allowed to the extent allocable to excluded income (IRC §911(d)(6)), and in higher-tax countries the foreign tax credit can beat the exclusion. Revoking the election bars a new election until the 6th taxable year after the revocation year without IRS consent (IRC §911(e)(2)). See the expat filing guide. Consult a tax professional to confirm eligibility.
