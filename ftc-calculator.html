Two strategies for US taxpayers abroad: The Foreign Earned Income Exclusion (FEIE, IRC §911) excludes foreign earned income up to an annual limit ($132,900 for 2026; 2027-2030 are projected). The Foreign Tax Credit (FTC, IRC §901) credits foreign income taxes against US tax, up to the §904 limitation, separately by income category (§904(d)). This calculator compares the two for the same facts, with the math shown step by step. Excluded income is stacked at the top of the brackets (§911(f)), and no credit is allowed for foreign tax on excluded income (§911(d)(6)).
