Basic Information

✗
W-2 employees cannot deduct home office expenses. Unreimbursed employee expenses are miscellaneous itemized deductions, which are suspended for tax years after 2017 and, as amended by P.L. 119-21 §70110, no longer have an end date. IRC §67(h). Only self-employed individuals (Schedule C, Schedule F) and partners with unreimbursed partnership expenses may claim this deduction. If you are a W-2 employee, this calculator does not apply to you.
Drives the SALT cap, the Social Security wage base and the carryover year.
Used exclusively and regularly for business. IRC §280A(c)(1). Simplified method counts at most 300 sq ft.
Total area of the home (all floors). Form 8829 line 2.
Schedule C line 29 (tentative profit), i.e. gross income from the business use of the home less other business expenses. Form 8829 line 8. Enter 0 for a loss. IRC §280A(c)(5).
If it began this year, expenses are prorated and first-year depreciation uses the month table. Count a month for the simplified method only with 15 or more days of use. Rev. Proc. 2013-13, sec. 4.08(4).
Renters deduct the business share of rent (Form 8829 line 19) and take no depreciation.
