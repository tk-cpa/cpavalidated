NOL Information

NOLs from tax years beginning before January 1, 2018. Carried forward 20 years, deductible up to 100% of taxable income, and used first. IRC §172(a)(2)(A), (b)(1)(A)(ii)(I), (b)(2).
NOLs from tax years beginning after December 31, 2017. Indefinite carryforward; deduction limited to 80% of taxable income. IRC §172(a)(2)(B), (b)(1)(A)(ii)(II). Individuals track carryovers on Form 172.
Loss year plus 20. Any unused pre-2018 balance is lost after this year. Enter the earliest expiring vintage if you have several.
For the first year of the schedule, computed without regard to the NOL deduction and the §199A and §250 deductions. Enter zero if the year is a loss year, and add that new NOL to the balance in the following year. IRC §172(a)(2)(B)(ii)(I), (d).
Your assumption for later years. Zero is allowed. This is not an inflation index.
Individual rates: IRC §1(j). Corporate rate: IRC §11(b). A flat rate is applied to every year.
An ownership change is a more than 50 percentage point increase in ownership by 5-percent shareholders over the testing period. IRC §382(g)(1).
Value of the old loss corporation x long-term tax-exempt rate (published by the IRS monthly). IRC §382(b)(1), (f). Unused limitation carries forward. Applies to all NOLs entered, treated as pre-change losses.
