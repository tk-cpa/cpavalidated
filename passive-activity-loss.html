Tax Year & Filing Status

Married filing separately and living together at any time during the year: no $25,000 allowance. IRC §469(i)(5)(B). Lived apart all year: $12,500 allowance and $50,000 phase-out start. IRC §469(i)(5)(A).
2

Activity Type & Amounts

A rental activity is passive even if you materially participate, unless you are a real estate professional. IRC §469(c)(2), (c)(7).
Enter as a positive number. The loss after basis and at-risk limits, before Section 469. Blank or negative counts as zero.
Disallowed losses carried to the next year as deductions of the same activity. IRC §469(b). Form 8582 Part IV or V, column (c).
Passive losses offset passive income first. IRC §469(a), (d)(1). Enter net income only; if your other passive activities are net losses, enter zero and add those losses above.
AGI without passive activity losses, taxable Social Security, IRA deductions and certain other items. IRC §469(i)(3)(E); Form 8582 instructions, line 6. Used only for the rental allowance.
Active participation is a lower standard than material participation. An interest under 10% by value, or a limited partner interest, does not qualify. IRC §469(i)(6)(A), (C). Rental real estate only.

Material Participation Tests  •  Temp. Reg. 1.469-5T(a)

Check all tests that apply to this activity. Meeting any one of the seven tests is material participation. For a non-rental business that makes it non-passive. For a rental it matters only if you are also a real estate professional (below). Your spouse's participation counts as yours. Temp. Reg. §1.469-5T(f)(3).

Real estate professional (rental real estate only). Both statutory requirements must be met, then you must still materially participate in the rental itself. IRC §469(c)(7)(A)-(B).
