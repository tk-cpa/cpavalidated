How to use this checker

A passive foreign investment company (PFIC) is a foreign corporation that meets the income test (75 percent or more of gross income is passive) or the asset test (average passive assets of at least 50 percent) in IRC §1297(a). Complete Sections 1 and 2 for status and filing, then the section for the regime that applies to you. The tool estimates; it does not choose a regime for you, because the three regimes tax different amounts in different years and the right choice depends on facts outside this tool. Individuals only (the highest rate table is IRC §1 rates).

PFIC Status Tests  •  IRC §1297

Income test
Income of a kind that is foreign personal holding company income under IRC §954(c), subject to the exceptions in §1297(b)(2) (active bank, qualifying insurance corporation, certain related-person payments, export trade income).
PFIC if passive income is 75 percent or more of gross income. IRC §1297(a)(1).
Asset test
Publicly traded corporations measure assets by value. IRC §1297(e)(1)(A), (e)(3).
A CFC that is not publicly traded measures assets by adjusted basis. IRC §1297(e)(2)(A).
Available only if not publicly traded. IRC §1297(e)(2)(B).
Assets that produce, or are held to produce, passive income. Use the average over the taxable year. IRC §1297(a)(2).
PFIC if the average percentage of passive assets is at least 50 percent.
Overlap and look-back rules
CFC overlap rule: not a PFIC as to you during the qualified portion of your holding period. IRC §1297(d).
Once-a-PFIC rule: the stock stays PFIC stock unless a purging election is made. IRC §1298(b)(1).

Purging and Related Elections

If the entity is a PFIC that is not a QEF, the stock stays PFIC stock for the rest of your holding period even in years when the tests are not met (IRC §1298(b)(1)). Elections on Form 8621 Part II can address this: a deemed sale election (IRC §1291(d)(2)(A); Treas. Reg. §1.1291-10), a deemed dividend election for a CFC (IRC §1291(d)(2)(B)), and the elections for former PFICs and section 1297(e) PFICs (Form 8621, Part II, Elections F, G and H). A QEF election is made on or before the due date, with extensions, of the return for the first year it is to apply (IRC §1295(b)(2)); a retroactive election is available only to the extent the regulations allow where the shareholder reasonably believed the company was not a PFIC. Each of these has conditions and consequences that this tool does not model.
