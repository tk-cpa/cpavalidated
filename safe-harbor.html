Tax Information

Required annual payment = the lesser of 90% of this year's tax or 100% of last year's tax (110% when last year's AGI exceeds $150,000, or $75,000 if married filing separately). IRC §6654(d)(1). Use the tax shown on the return (Form 1040, line 24 basis). Withholding counts toward the total. The prior-year test applies only if last year was a full 12-month year with a filed return. These thresholds are fixed by statute and are not inflation-indexed, so the federal rules are the same for every year from 2026 through 2030.
