S-Corp Reasonable Compensation & Tax Savings 2026-2030

Compare payroll and income tax for a sole proprietor against an S-corp shareholder-employee, tax years 2026-2030. Models the Social Security wage base by year, Additional Medicare Tax by filing status, and the 20% QBI deduction. Federal only; state is a flat rate you enter.

Tax year & filing status

2026 uses the Social Security Administration published wage base. 2027-2030 are projections.
Sets the Additional Medicare Tax threshold: $200,000 (single, head of household), $250,000 (joint), $125,000 (separate). IRC 3101(b)(2); IRC 1401(b)(2)(A). Amounts are fixed by statute and not indexed.
Used only for years after 2026. The wage base is the 1994 base of $60,600 times the national average wage index ratio, rounded to the nearest $300 (Social Security Administration, contribution and benefit base). Default 2.5%.
2

Business & owner information

Profit before any owner compensation and before employer payroll tax. Sole proprietor: Schedule C net profit. S-corp: the amount split between salary and distributions.
W-2 wages paid to the shareholder-employee. Must be reasonable for services rendered: Rev. Rul. 74-44; David E. Watson, P.C. v. United States, 668 F.3d 1008 (8th Cir. 2012).
Wages from other employers. They use up the owner's Social Security wage base (IRC 1402(b)(1)) and count toward the Additional Medicare threshold.
Counts toward the $250,000 joint Additional Medicare threshold: IRC 1401(b)(2)(B); IRC 3101(b)(2).
Rates under IRC 1(j)(2) for 2026 (Rev. Proc. 2025-32) and, by statute, later years. Applied to the change in taxable income; the tool does not run the full return.
Flat marginal rate on business income. Enter 0 for a state with no income tax. Entity-level state taxes are not modeled.
IRC 199A is permanent after P.L. 119-21 section 70105. Reasonable compensation is excluded from qualified business income: IRC 199A(c)(4)(A). The deduction is applied to federal tax only.
Payroll service, Form 1120-S preparation, state fees. Enter your own quote; it is subtracted from the S-corp result.
