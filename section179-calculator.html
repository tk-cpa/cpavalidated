Property & Election

Include all eligible property placed in service, not only what you elect to expense: it drives the phase-out. 26 U.S.C. 179(b)(2); Form 4562, line 2. Business-use cost only (Treas. Reg. 1.179-1(d)).
SUV = 4-wheeled vehicle rated 14,000 lbs GVW or less and not subject to 280F (excludes vehicles seating more than 9 behind the driver, with a 6-foot open cargo bed, or fully enclosed cargo vans). 179(b)(5)(B).
