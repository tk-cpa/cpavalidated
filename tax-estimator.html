Projected figures are estimates. For 2027-2030 the Internal Revenue Service has not published inflation adjustments. Bracket thresholds, standard deductions and capital gain breakpoints are projected from 2026 at the inflation rate entered, rounding the increase down to the next lowest $50 ($25 for married filing separately brackets) as IRC §1(f)(7) requires. The statute indexes from a base year, so actual amounts can differ by a rounding step. Amounts fixed by statute (the SALT cap, the senior deduction, net investment income tax thresholds, the 37% bracket rule) are exact.
State figures. State estimates apply the 2026 rates, brackets, standard deductions and exemptions compiled by the Tax Foundation (as of February 11, 2026) to federal AGI, and hold them constant for 2027-2030. Verify with the state revenue department before relying on any figure.
