Income Profile (Selected Tax Year)

Under the mirror code, a bona fide USVI resident files one income tax return with the Virgin Islands Bureau of Internal Revenue reporting income from all sources, and that income is excluded from U.S. gross income when the conditions of IRC §932(c)(4) are met. The EDC reduction applies only to tax attributable to income from Virgin Islands sources or income effectively connected with a Virgin Islands trade or business (IRC §934(b)(1)). All amounts are in U.S. dollars for the year selected above.

A corporation is taxed at the entity level at 21% (IRC §11(b)) plus the 10% Virgin Islands corporate surtax. A pass-through owner is taxed on the distributive share at individual rates.
90% for certificate holders (29 V.I.C. §713b(a), (b)); the Commission may approve a lesser percentage (§713a(b)). Capped at 90% here.
Whether the net investment income tax applies to bona fide USVI residents is disputed (secondary source). The conservative choice applies it to dividends and other investment income. The 90% reduction is not applied to the NIIT. IRC §1411.
C corporation: net taxable income of the entity. Pass-through: your distributive share, taxed to you whether or not distributed.
Income from sources outside the Virgin Islands, or effectively connected with a U.S. trade or business, is outside the reduction. IRC §934(b)(1). Enter 0 if all income is Virgin Islands-source.
Assumed qualified dividends (0/15/20%). C corporation only; ignored for a pass-through, whose distributions of taxed income are not taxed again. Owner reduction: 29 V.I.C. §713b(e)(2).
Wages, interest, rents, and other income outside the EDC business. Taxed on the Virgin Islands return at the regular mirror-code rates with no reduction (IRC §934(a)). Treated as not net investment income.
Taxed at 0/15/20% (IRC §1(h)) with no reduction. Treated as net investment income.

Washington taxes long-term capital gains only (excise tax), so a Washington selection applies this custom rate to the long-term gains field only.
Mirror code (48 U.S.C. §1397; IRC §932): The Virgin Islands does not write its own income tax code. The Internal Revenue Code applies in the Virgin Islands with the Virgin Islands substituted for the United States, and the proceeds go to the Virgin Islands treasury. IRC §934(a) bars reducing that tax except as §934(b) allows, and §934(b)(1) allows a reduction only for tax attributable to Virgin Islands-source or effectively connected income. The Legislature may levy a surtax of up to 10% (48 U.S.C. §1397); corporations pay it on Form 1120ES-VI.
