Depreciation Elections

Drives the bonus rate: 100% if acquired after January 19, 2025. Earlier acquisitions: 20% for 2026, none after 2026. Rev. Proc. 2026-15, sec. 2.02.
Mid-quarter applies only if more than 40% of the year's MACRS basis (excluding real property) was placed in service in Q4. 168(d)(3).
Passenger autos: 179 counts against the 280F cap. SUVs: limited to the 179(b)(5) cap. Overall 179 limits are assumed not to bind (see the Section 179 calculator).
The election out applies to the whole 5-year class placed in service that year. 168(k)(7).
